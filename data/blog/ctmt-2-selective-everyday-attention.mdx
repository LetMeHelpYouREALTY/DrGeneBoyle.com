## Direct answer

[@Boyle2024CTMT] reported **significant correlations** between CTMT-2 performance and Test of Everyday Attention for Children–Second Edition (**TEA-Ch2**) **Selective Attention Index** and **Everyday Attention Index** scores. Multiple regression analyses indicated that CTMT-2 trails **significantly predicted** both TEA-Ch2 index scores (*N* = 30, ages 8–15).

## Takeaway

The abstract concludes that findings support the CTMT-2’s utility in assessing **selective attention** and **everyday attention** (along with processing speed) in this school-aged sample—positioning CTMT-2 as a potential **supplemental** tool within broader attentional assessment.
