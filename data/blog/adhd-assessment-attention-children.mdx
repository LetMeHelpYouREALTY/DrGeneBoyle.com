## Direct answer

Accurate assessment of attentional deficits is crucial for **ADHD diagnosis and treatment planning** in children and adolescents [@Boyle2024CTMT]. Boyle (2024) examined the CTMT-2 alongside WISC-V, CAt-C, and TEA-Ch2 in ages 8–15 (*N* = 30) and concluded the CTMT-2 may be a **valuable supplemental tool**—not a stand-alone diagnostic substitute.

## Practice framing (from the abstract)

- Pair trail-making performance with validated cognitive, symptom-rating, and everyday-attention measures.
- Interpret CTMT-2 results in a **comprehensive** assessment battery.
- Future work should test diverse clinical samples and links to targeted interventions.

Educational content only—not individualized clinical advice.
