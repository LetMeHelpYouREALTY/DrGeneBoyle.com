## Direct answer

**Serious mental illness (SMI)** generally refers to mental disorders that substantially interfere with major life activities. Exact definitions vary by study, payer, and public system; in psychiatric rehabilitation and motherhood research, SMI denotes conditions with significant functional impact—not a single diagnosis code.

## Why the definition matters

When writers say “women with SMI,” they are usually pointing to a **functional** category: illness severe enough to disrupt roles such as work, relationships, housing stability, or parenting. That framing is why [@Mizock2019] could interview both mothers and non-mothers—the research question was the relationship to the motherhood *role*, not only current custody status.
