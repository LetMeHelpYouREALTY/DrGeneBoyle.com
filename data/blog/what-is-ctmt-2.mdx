## Direct answer

The **Comprehensive Trail Making Test-Second Edition (CTMT-2)** is a standardized neuropsychological test built from five timed visual search and sequencing “trails.” Performance is heavily influenced by attention, concentration, resistance to distraction, and cognitive flexibility (set-shifting).

## Why it appears on this site

[@Boyle2024CTMT] studied whether CTMT-2 performance relates to cognitive abilities, inattention ratings, and attentional capacities in school-aged children and adolescents (*N* = 30, ages 8–15). Read the [dissertation research brief](/blog/ctmt-attention-trail-making-dissertation) for sample, measures, and findings.
