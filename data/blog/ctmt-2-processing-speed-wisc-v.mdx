## Direct answer

In [@Boyle2024CTMT], CTMT-2 performance showed **significant correlations** with Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children–Fifth Edition (**WISC-V**) **Processing Speed Index (PSI)** scores. Multiple regression analyses indicated that **CTMT-2 trails significantly predicted PSI scores** in a sample of school-aged children and adolescents (*N* = 30, ages 8–15).

## Why processing speed matters here

Processing speed indices capture how quickly a child completes timed cognitive tasks. Trail-making tests also depend on speeded visual search and sequencing. Boyle’s abstract places PSI among the cognitive ability outcomes tied to CTMT-2 performance—alongside TEA-Ch2 attention indices.
