## Direct answer

**Eugene Boyle, Ph.D.** (Boyle, Eugene) completed a Fielding Graduate University doctoral dissertation dated **2024-12-02** on the utility of the CTMT-2 for assessing attentional deficits in children and adolescents [@Boyle2024CTMT].
