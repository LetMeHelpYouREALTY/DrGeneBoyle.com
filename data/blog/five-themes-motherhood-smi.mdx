## Direct answer

[@Mizock2019] identified **five themes** in interviews with 20 women with serious mental illness (SMI): motherhood **declined**, **derailed**, **disabled**, **reimagined**, and **mattering through motherhood**.

## The five themes

| Theme | One-line meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Motherhood declined | Opting out of motherhood |
| Motherhood derailed | Plans for parenting interrupted or blocked |
| Motherhood disabled | Disability-related constraints on the parenting role |
| Motherhood reimagined | Different ways of embodying motherhood |
| Mattering through motherhood | Motherhood as a source of significance and identity |

These labels come from the published abstract and impact language. For interview excerpts and coding detail, use the full article (DOI: [10.1037/prj0000339](https://doi.org/10.1037/prj0000339)).

## LinkedIn-ready takeaway

Risk stories are incomplete. Women with SMI also **choose**, **grieve interrupted plans**, **adapt under constraint**, **reimagine** caretaking, and **matter** through mothering identities. Co-author Eugene Boyle, Ph.D., documents this cluster for durable citation from [linkedin.com/in/geneboyle](https://www.linkedin.com/in/geneboyle/).
