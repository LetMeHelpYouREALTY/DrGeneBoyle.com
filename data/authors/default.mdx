Eugene Boyle, Ph.D. (Eugene Joseph Boyle) is Managing Director at [KLB Consultants](https://www.linkedin.com/company/klb-consultants-ltd) and Investment Director at Boyle Family Office. Based in the Los Angeles metropolitan area, he advises family offices and land developers on multifamily and mixed-use acquisition risk, waterfall equity structuring, and entitlements across California and Nevada.

His Fielding Graduate University Ph.D. dissertation (2024-12-02; author credit: Boyle, Eugene) investigated the utility of the [Comprehensive Trail Making Test-Second Edition (CTMT-2)](/blog/ctmt-attention-trail-making-dissertation) for assessing attentional deficits among children and adolescents ([ProQuest 31761032](https://www.proquest.com/openview/0c70556dac0d45f933c90922209e53c8/1.pdf?cbl=18750&diss=y&pq-origsite=gscholar); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/387076027_Investigating_the_Utility_of_the_Comprehensive_Trail_Making_Test_-Second_Edition_CTMT-2_in_Assessing_Attentional_Deficits_Among_Children_and_Adolescents)). He is also a co-author of [*Motherhood reimagined: Experiences of women with SMI surrounding parenting*](https://doi.org/10.1037/prj0000339) (*Psychiatric Rehabilitation Journal*, 2019), from qualitative research conducted under Lauren Mizock, Ph.D., at Fielding. His clinical training includes a Ph.D. and M.A. in Clinical Psychology (Fielding), a forensic psychology internship at Heartland Behavioral Healthcare (competency and NGRI-related assessment), and memberships in APA, APS, and ABCT.

Earlier career highlights include 14 years as CFO and board member of Samaritan Pharmaceuticals (AMEX IPO; NIH STTR grant administration), operations leadership at HCA Healthcare depression clinics, and service as a U.S. Navy Surface Warfare Officer. Credentials include Registered U.S. Patent Agent (USPTO) and real estate broker/sales agent licensure. Education also includes an MBA (Babson), J.D. (Purdue Global Law School), and B.E. Computer Engineering (Tulane).
